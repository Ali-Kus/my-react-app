Hinokami Kagura

Swing your mouse like a katana. It leaves a curved arc of fire along the path it just travelled, white-hot at the tip and breaking into flame tongues along the trailing edge.

The native cursor is hidden. Everything below stays clickable and scrollable — the fire renders on a pass-through overlay, and the page is empty until you move.

Move fast for a long sweeping slash; slow down and it pulls back to a small blade flame at the cursor.
